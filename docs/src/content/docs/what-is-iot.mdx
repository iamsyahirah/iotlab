The **Internet of Things (IoT)** refers to physical devices that are connected to the internet and can collect, send, or receive data.

Examples of IoT devices include:

- Smart home devices
- Temperature monitoring systems
- Smart farming systems
- Wearable devices
- Smart street lights
- Security systems

## How IoT Works

A simple IoT system normally consists of four main parts:

1. **Sensor**  
   Collects information from the environment.

2. **Microcontroller**  
   Processes the data received from the sensor.

3. **Internet Connection**  
   Sends or receives data through Wi-Fi or another communication method.

4. **IoT Platform**  
   Displays the data and allows the user to monitor or control the system.

## Simple Example

Imagine a smart farm system.

A soil moisture sensor measures the amount of water in the soil.

The ESP32 reads the sensor value and sends the information to an IoT platform through Wi-Fi.

If the soil becomes too dry, the system can automatically turn on a water pump.
